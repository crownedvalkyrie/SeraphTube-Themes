# 🎨 SeraphTube Themes

Welcome to **SeraphTube Themes**, a collection of custom themes made for my Rust and Tauri-based YouTube wrapper for Macs!

✨ Looking to change up the look of SeraphTube? Here you'll find extra themes that you can download and install to customize your experience beyond the 3 built-in themes.

📦 All themes are made to give you more ways to personalize your experience without being limited to the default options.

🚀 **More themes will be added over time**, so check back for new designs and updates! A fair warning that some themes might not look good (some of these I made in a rush lol).

Enjoy customizing! ❤️
